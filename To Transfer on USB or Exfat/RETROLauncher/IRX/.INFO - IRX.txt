IRX - IOP drivers
=================

Every IOP driver the launcher ships, in one folder.

    ata_bd.irx      internal ATA disk as a BDM device -> massN:
    dev9_ns.irx     the expansion bay the ATA port sits behind
    usbd.irx        POPStarter's USB driver
    usbhdfsd.irx    POPStarter's FAT driver

Two different jobs, one folder
------------------------------
The first two are loaded into the IOP at boot, in the order listed in
IRX_ORDEN (system.lua), so that RETROLauncher can see an internal exFAT disk.
Enceladus reloads its own BDM stack at startup WITHOUT ata_bd, which is exactly
why the disk has to be re-attached here.

The last two are never loaded. They are copied to mc0:/POPSTARTER/ when
POPStarter needs them - and copied in LOWERCASE, which is the whole point:
POPStarter opens them by lowercase name and reports "No POPS directory" if it
finds USBD.IRX instead of usbd.irx. That single detail cost an evening.

They are listed in IRX_IGNORAR so the boot scanner skips them: Enceladus has
already loaded its own USB stack, and loading a second one would be at best
useless.

Adding a driver
---------------
Drop the .irx here and it is loaded at boot, after the ones in IRX_ORDEN. To
ship one without loading it, add its name to IRX_IGNORAR in system.lua.

Neutrino keeps its own modules in Neutrino/modules/ - those belong to its
package and it looks for them there, so leave them where they are.
